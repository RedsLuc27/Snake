# A custom snake
Here is my snake game. 
You are a snake (like the real game), but with a little twist. 

# The twist:
Each time you eat an apple you lose control over your snake, and the games goes faster and faster!

From 1 to 9 apples the games is completely normal, and goes faster 0.5 time each time you eat an apple.
From 10 to 19 apples, if you press your key 10 times only 8 will be counted. 
From 20 to 29 apples, if you press your key 10 times only 6 will be counted. 
From 30 to 49 apples, if you press your key 10 times only 4 will be counted. 
From 50 to infinte apples, if you press your key 10 times only 2 will be counted.
